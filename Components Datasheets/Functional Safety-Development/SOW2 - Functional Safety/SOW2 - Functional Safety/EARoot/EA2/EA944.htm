Project:
  • Behavior
  • Delayed Video frames older than FHTI (see REQ 24548584-76) from actual traffic situation shall not be transmitted
  • Fault Handling Time Interval (FHTI) shall be 400 ms
  • Frozen Video frames older than FHTI (see REQ 24548584-76) from actual traffic situation shall not be transmitted
  • Transmission of flickered frames shall be avoided
  • Transmission of frames containing wrong field of view shall be avoided
  • Transmission of frozen frame shall be avoided
  • Transmission of too bright frames shall be avoided
  • Transmission of too dim frames shall be avoided
  • Transmission of too low contrast frames shall be avoided
  • Transmission of too low frame rate frames shall be avoided
  • Transmission of too much noisy frames shall be avoided
  • Transmission of video frames older than 50ms shall be avoided
  • Transmission of wrong resolution frames shall be avoided
  • Video frames causing an unclear view (too dim, too low contrast, too much disturbances or noise, wrong resolution,flicker, too low frame rate) shall not be transmitted
  • Video frames presenting wrong field of view (eg cropped/partial images) not easily detected by the driver shall not betransmitted
  • Video frames shall be transmitted within 50 ms from actual traffic situation.
